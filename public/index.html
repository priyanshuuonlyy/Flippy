Free Random Video Chat, Designed Around Gestures

Flippy is a free browser-based video chat experience for meeting new people. Hand gestures let you move to another conversation or send visual reactions. Camera activity checks help pause unusable video feeds; they do not verify identity or prevent every inappropriate interaction.

Camera-Ready Matching

CameraGate checks basic frame activity and lighting before matching. These checks are not identity verification and cannot guarantee that every feed is genuine.

Respectful Conversations

Flippy is built for friendly, respectful conversations. Be considerate and avoid sharing personal details with people you have just met.

Touchless Hand Gestures

Control your conversation hands-free! A quick two-finger swipe or double scissor snip (✂️) skips immediately to someone new. A two-handed heart (🫶) showers your partner with love reactions.

Completely Free & Private

No account or payment is currently required. Calls use encrypted WebRTC; relay servers may assist connectivity. Flippy does not provide a server-side call recording feature.

Community Expectations & Safety Development

✓ Camera Activity Checks ✓ Respectful Conduct Rules ✓ Free Conversations ✓ Instant Skip Gesture ✓ Peer-to-Peer Encrypted

Nudity, harassment, coercion and abusive behavior are not welcome on Flippy. Camera activity checks are not a substitute for content moderation. Doer is developing reporting, enforcement and age-appropriate safeguards; these are not yet verified as active protections.

What CameraGate Checks

Before matching, CameraGate evaluates basic frame activity and lighting. A black, inactive or unusable feed may pause matching. If a conversation makes you uncomfortable, use Next or Leave to disconnect. Identity verification and automatic explicit-content detection are not provided by CameraGate.

Respect for Everyone

Every user deserves respectful treatment. Random video chat involves strangers, so no platform can guarantee someone’s behavior. Please protect your privacy and disconnect if you encounter unwanted conduct.

Frequently Asked Questions About Flippy

Is Flippy completely free to use?

Yes! Flippy is 100% free random video chat. There are no paid tiers, subscriptions, coins, credits, or hidden charges. You can enjoy unlimited face-to-face video conversations at zero cost.

What safety features does Flippy currently have?

CameraGate monitors video activity and can pause matchmaking for unusable feeds. Next and Leave let you exit a conversation. Live content moderation, user reporting, age-appropriate matching and enforcement improvements are being developed; do not assume they already protect every call.

Can I use Flippy on mobile devices (Android and iPhone)?

Yes! Flippy is fully optimized for mobile browsers, including Chrome on Android and Safari on iOS. No app download is required — simply visit the website on your phone, grant camera and microphone access, and start chatting immediately.

How do the hand gesture shortcuts work?

Flippy uses on-device computer vision to detect natural gestures: hold your index and middle fingers together and sweep sideways, or snip fingers twice in place like a pair of scissors (✂️) to skip to the next person. Bring your hands together into a heart shape to send floating love balloons.

Are my video calls or personal details recorded?

Flippy uses encrypted WebRTC connections. When direct connectivity is unavailable, a relay may carry traffic. The current application does not offer server-side call recording. Your conversation partner may still record their screen, so avoid sharing sensitive information.
