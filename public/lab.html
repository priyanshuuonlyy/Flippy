02 / Your gesture check

  1. Start two partners, then open camera practice.
  2. Enable your real camera and start a chat.
  3. Hold two fingers still: no skip should happen.
  4. Swipe horizontally: expect one new partner.
  5. Make a two-hand heart: expect one burst.
  6. Open test diagnostics and export your report.

Repeat without gestures, while talking, touching your face and moving naturally. An unwanted skip matters more than a missed decorative reaction.
